Alton Brown's Aged Egg Nog

Ingredients

  • 12 Egg yolks
  • 8oz whole milk
  • 1 pint whole milk (-8oz)
  • 1 Pint Half and Half
  • 1 pint heavy cream
  • 1 cup Rum (Jamaican) (43abv)
  • 1 cup Cognac (45abv)
  • 1 cup Bourbon (50abv)
  • 1lb sugar
  • 1 tsp Nutmeg

Steps

Phase One

  1. seperate egg yolks, freeze whites in an ice cube tray to save for other uses.
  2. In a seperate bowl, combine the rum, congnac, and bourbon.
  3. Add 8ox of milk to the egg yolks. Add 2/3 tsp nutmeg and Sugar. Mix with an immersion blender on low. Avoid pulling air into the mixture.
  4. Once mixture is smooth, slowly add the alcohol while blending.
  5. store in a clear glass jar, tightly sealed. Store in the fridge for 30 days. Shake gently every few days.

Phase 2

  1. Pour misture into a mixing container. Add 2 cups half and half, 2 cups heavy cream, and 1 cup whole milk.
  2. Add 1/3 tsp nutmeg. Blend well on low with immersion blender.
  3. transfer into 4 1 quart jars, seal tightly
  4. Store in the fridge for at least 60 days. Up to 6 months, after that flavours start to mellow.
