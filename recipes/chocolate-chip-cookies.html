"The Chewie" Chocolate Chip Cookie

Ingredients

  • 227g Butter (2 sticks)
  • 225g Light Brown Sugar
  • 60g Sugar
  • 310g Bread Flour
  • 3g Powdered Milk
  • 6g Kosher Salt
  • 5g Baking Powder
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Egg yolk
  • 7g (1 1/2tsp) vanilla extract
  • 45g Water
  • 340g Chocolate Chips

Steps

  1. Cut the butter into slices and melt in a medium pot over low heat.
  2. Once Melted, raise to medium heat (too hot can cause splashes). Butter will start to bubble as water boils out. Bubbles will start to stack up on top of each other. At this point reduce heat and stir gently with a silicone brush. When you start to smell nuttiness, remove from heat. Bubbles should be smaller and color should start to change.
  3. Combine sugar and brown sugar in a mixing bowl. Add browned butter and mix with paddle attachement on low speed and add b
  4. in a seperate bowl combine flour, milk powder, salt, and baking soda.
  5. beat the egg, egg yolk, vanilla extract, and water together just until mixed.
  6. SLOWLY add the egg mixture to the sugar, slightly increasing mixer speed.
  7. Once smooth, slowly add the dry ingredients in 3 doses. With everything combined add the chocolate chips.
  8. Dish onto a baking sheet with parchment paper in 60g balls. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate overnight.
  9. Bake straight from fridge in an oven at 350F. Optionally finish cookies with a finishing salt before baking. Bake 6-7 minutes, rotate the pan, and bake another 7-8 minutes. Let cool on a rack
